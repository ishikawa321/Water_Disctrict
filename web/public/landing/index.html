Do consumers need the desktop app?

No. Consumers can use the mobile app or consumer web portal. Authorized Admin and Staff use the office workspaces.

How are Admin and Staff different?

Admins manage service accounts, bills, verified offline payment records, and announcements. They also review concerns and create and assign repair tickets. Staff access focuses on concern review and updates to their own assigned repair work.

Can I pay my water bill on this landing page?

No. This page showcases the project. The current system records confirmed offline payments; it does not process online payments. All balances and records in this page’s tour are fictional examples.

Are the previews actual application screens?

The previews are illustrative layouts made for this landing page, based on features documented in the project branches. They are not screenshots or live account data. Ask the team at the exhibit for a demonstration of the applications.

Is Water District Connect an official public service?

This is a student-developed project showcase for a tech exhibit. This landing page does not represent an official service launch or offer a district service guarantee.
